# Code of conduct

Octachrome is a place for people who like making music with machines. Be the kind of person you would want to share a studio with.

* **Be kind and patient.** Assume good faith. People arrive with different experience of Rust, of music and of hardware.
* **Be specific and generous in review.** Criticise the work, not the person; say what would make it better.
* **No harassment or discrimination** of any kind, and no personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere the project is represented.
* **Keep private things private:** do not publish other people's personal information.

**If something goes wrong**, report it privately to the maintainer through the repository's *Security → Report a vulnerability* form (it is also open for conduct reports) or by opening an issue that asks for a private conversation, without details. The maintainer can remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules.

This code applies to the project's spaces and to the people who speak for it elsewhere.
