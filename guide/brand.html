The mark

Eight keys as the unit has them, four and four, or eight knobs in a row. Red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet, white. Always.

The infinity logo

The same eight, same order. The 4 + 4 keys and the knob row are the mark. The infinity arrangement is an alternate: an 8 on its side.

8va

In music, 8va is the octave: the eighth note is the first one again, higher. Seven colours, then the white that comes back.

Tap a key. The white is the first note, one octave up.
